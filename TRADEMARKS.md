# SlideMarks name and logo

"SlideMarks" and the Harvey-ball logo (a three-quarter filled circle) identify the official SlideMarks font, Office add-in and website, published by Patrick O'Leary at:

- https://github.com/pjaol/SlideMarks
- https://pjaol.github.io/SlideMarks/

The licences (Apache 2.0 for the code, SIL OFL 1.1 for the font) let you use, modify and redistribute the work. They do **not** grant rights to the name or logo:
- Apache 2.0, section 6: no permission to use the licensor's trade names or marks.
- SIL OFL: "SlideMarks" is a Reserved Font Name.

## You may
- Say that your product or document *uses* or *is compatible with* SlideMarks.
- Redistribute **unmodified** official releases under the name SlideMarks.
- Refer to the project by name in articles, tutorials and reviews.

## Please don't
- Publish a modified font, add-in, website or package under the name **SlideMarks**, or a confusingly similar name, or with the logo. Forks are welcome under a different name.
- Publish an Office add-in, browser extension or installer that presents itself as SlideMarks, or as official, when it isn't.
- Suggest endorsement by the project without permission.

If you've found a copy pretending to be official, please open an issue: https://github.com/pjaol/SlideMarks/issues
