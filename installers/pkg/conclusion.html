SlideMarks is installed

  1. Quit Word and PowerPoint (⌘Q) and reopen them. Office only sees new fonts and add-ins when it starts.
  2. Click Add-ins on the Home tab and pick SlideMarks (under Developer Add-ins in Word).

If symbols show as empty boxes, quit Office once more: it sometimes needs a second restart to load a new font.

To uninstall, run this in Terminal:
curl -fsSL {{BASE_URL}}/install.sh | sh -s uninstall
