SlideMarks {{VERSION}}

Presentation symbols that behave like text: Harvey balls, arrows, checks, ratings and trends for PowerPoint and Word.

This installs two things, for your user account only:

  • The SlideMarks font, which draws the symbols.
    ~/Library/Fonts/SlideMarks-Regular.ttf
  • The SlideMarks add-in for Word and PowerPoint, a panel for finding and inserting symbols. It's added to each app you've opened at least once.

Free and open source. No accounts and no tracking: the add-in collects no data.

macOS will ask two things:

  • your password (or fingerprint), as for any installer. Nothing is installed outside your own Library folder.
  • whether Installer can “access data from other apps”. Choose Allow: Word and PowerPoint only load add-ins from a folder inside their own app data.

When it's done, the SlideMarks help page opens with the next steps.
